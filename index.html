Next gen infrastructure for real time interactions

Led by Jean-Baptiste Kempf (CEO of Kyber & President of VideoLAN), Kyber is his new venture to solve the latency problem once and for all.

kyber.tech

8MS
Lowest latency

Below perception

8–10 ms glass-to-glass. Below the human hand-eye loop.

The vision

No delay, anywhere

A world where life-saving robot surgeries, drones or immersive agents can be remotely operated with no delay. The goal: “near-human latency” (< 50 ms), so machines feel like they are at your fingertips.

Technology stack

Protocol-level

A patented network protocol built on QUIC and WebTransport. The core builds on VLC and FFmpeg open-source technology, using Rust and WebAssembly for high-performance browser integration.
